Privacy Policy

Daily-Tasks · effective 9 September 2026

Summary

Daily-Tasks is a desktop application that runs entirely on your own computer. It contains no analytics, telemetry, ads, or trackers, and it sends none of your data to the developer. Your tasks stay on your device unless you choose to enable optional Google Drive sync.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps all of its data locally in the ~/Daily-Tasks/ directory:

  • tasks.json — your tasks (text, dates, completion/reminder state).
  • config.json — app preferences such as your light/dark theme.
  • sync.json — non-secret sync settings (the name of your rclone remote and the remote path). It contains no passwords or tokens.

This information never leaves your computer except through the optional sync described below.

Optional Google Drive sync

Cloud sync is off by default. If you turn it on, it works like this:

  • Sync uses rclone as the transport. Your Google sign-in is handled entirely by rclone during a one-time rclone config. The app never sees, receives, or stores your Google password.
  • Your rclone authorization token is stored by rclone in ~/.config/rclone/rclone.conf on your own machine — outside the app's data directory, and it is never itself uploaded.
  • When enabled, only tasks.json is copied to a folder in your own Google Drive account (default Daily-Tasks/tasks.json). Your theme and other settings are not synced.
  • The developer has no access to your Google account or your synced data. Data you store in Google Drive is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

This website

This site is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or third-party trackers. As with any website, the hosting provider (GitHub) may process standard technical information such as your IP address in order to serve the page; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children's privacy

Daily-Tasks is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information.

Changes to this policy

Any updates will be published on this page with a new effective date.
